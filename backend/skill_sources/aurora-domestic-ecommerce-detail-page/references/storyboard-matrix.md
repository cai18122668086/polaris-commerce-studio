# Storyboard matrix

## Purpose

Approve the whole campaign's expression system before generating individual screens. Content order alone is not enough.

For every module record:

- duty and buyer question;
- lead conclusion and supporting facts;
- proof device and evidence class;
- visible content and forbidden inventions;
- composition family;
- camera scale;
- primary text mode and placement logic;
- reading path;
- copy density and visual-payload density as separate fields;
- visual proof contract: buyer takeaway, visual verb, primary/supporting subjects, required proof relations, specificity anchors, hidden-copy test and minimum effective visual payloads;
- proof-relation strength and commercial energy;
- exact visible-copy whitelist;
- brand-anchor treatment;
- inherited visual-DNA tokens;
- deliberate variation from the previous module.

## Composition families

Use the smallest family vocabulary that fits the campaign:

- `hero_anchor`;
- `immersive_scene`;
- `asymmetric_editorial`;
- `macro_material`;
- `comparison_split`;
- `process_sequence`;
- `structure_diagram`;
- `data_evidence`;
- `origin_story`;
- `sku_selection`;
- `specification_closure`.
- `directed_pathway`;
- `interaction_state`;
- `installation_fit`;
- `selection_matrix`.

Families are abstract geometry, not reusable page templates.

## Scheduling rules

- Do not repeat both the same composition family and the same primary text mode in adjacent modules unless a written transition reason makes the repetition purposeful.
- Alternate camera scale, copy density, visual-payload density and proof form across the page.
- Preserve at least two visible visual-DNA tokens in every module.
- A rational high-copy-density module should normally receive a lower-copy-density sensory, scene or result neighbor, while that neighbor still carries enough visual payload to communicate without its copy.
- Reserve high-impact headline-led structures for moments that deserve a stop; do not ban them.
- Page transitions must preserve the buyer's question flow, not merely visual alternation.
- For advanced archetypes, record the relation endpoints and downgrade route; for multi-entity pages, record hero/companion/accessory/variant/context-only roles.
- Audit every rolling three-module window across duty, visual carrier, camera scale, and primary text mode. Repetition on three or four axes requires an explicit story reason or a planned variation.

## User-facing approval

Show the user a concise matrix with:

`模块 | 页面职责 | 主结论 | 画面如何证明 | 构图与景别 | 文字如何进入画面 | 与前后屏关系`

Also show one campaign visual-DNA paragraph. Approval authorizes the campaign logic and expression matrix, not fixed coordinates.
