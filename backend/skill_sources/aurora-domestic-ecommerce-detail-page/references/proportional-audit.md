# Proportional visual audit

## Module audit order

Judge actual pixels in this order:

1. Identity: same product, variant, packaging, parts, count and physical relationships.
2. Facts: visible copy, numbers, conditions, certification and comparison.
3. Duty: intended buyer question and lead conclusion are recognizable.
4. Proof: current-product evidence visibly carries the lead claim.
5. Visual performance: product dominance, hierarchy, depth, finish, desire and scan order.
6. Proof integrity: visible contents, texture, ingredients, results, reports and comparisons match the declared evidence class.
7. Legibility: Chinese text, numeric hierarchy, contrast and safe area.
8. Text integration: placement follows the subject, action, evidence or negative space and does not default to an unjustified poster title.
9. Series continuity and logo scope: visual-DNA tokens remain in the same campaign; M01 may show one authorized independent logo, M02 onward show none, and package-printed logos remain natural product identity.
10. Page distinctness: composition, camera, text mode, reading path, copy density and visual-payload density differ purposefully from adjacent modules.
11. Hidden-copy comprehension: after covering all copy, the buyer can still identify the product-specific claim from subjects, actions and relations.
12. Pixel hygiene: no module IDs, page numbers, workflow labels, next-screen hints, claim IDs or planning vocabulary appear in the rendered image.
11. Integrity: no impossible geometry, broken hands/parts, source leakage or third-party marks.

Record concrete observed evidence for every result. Never audit from a prompt or filename.

## Material failures

Permit automatic repair only for:

- product identity or factual error;
- unsupported claim or fabricated proof;
- unreadable or corrupted essential Chinese;
- missing visual product evidence for a renderable lead claim;
- module duty no longer recognizable;
- impossible geometry or broken product relation;
- obvious source-background or third-party asset leakage;
- severe campaign discontinuity;
- any independent, floating, corner, header, footer, watermark or repeated logo on M02 onward;
- fabricated photorealistic product proof;
- essential text placed as an unrelated overlay that breaks the approved visual proof;
- mechanical repetition of both composition family and text mode when it materially collapses the approved long-page rhythm;
- critical seam content cut or width mismatch.

## Advisory issues

Do not spend the repair budget on:

- multiple related selling points;
- useful information bars;
- a stronger composition than the plan sketch;
- slight spacing, color or decoration preference;
- omitted optional information;
- non-identical but compatible layout families.

## Long-page audit

Inspect the assembled page for:

- first-screen stopping power;
- stable product identity end to end;
- real proof of core claims;
- purchase-decision order and natural transitions;
- repeated claims or proof forms;
- independent high/low copy-density and visual-payload rhythm;
- alternation among scene, material, mechanism, parameter and trust;
- one coherent visual world;
- typography-role and placement rhythm;
- brand-anchor continuity through visual DNA, with an independent logo only on M01 and none on M02 onward;
- truthful proof with no invented product states;
- quality retention in lower modules;
- final purchase closure;
- invisible or intentional seams.

The long-page audit records residual issues. It does not launch another multi-module repair loop.
