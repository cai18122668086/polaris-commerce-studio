# Core policy

## Non-negotiable hierarchy

Preserve in this order:

1. Product identity.
2. Factual and legal compliance.
3. Module role and buyer conclusion.
4. Visual proof of the lead value.
5. Commercial impact and desire.
6. Legibility.
7. Campaign continuity.
8. Decoration.

Never trade a higher item for a lower one.

## Two user checkpoints

Ask the user to intervene only at:

1. Approval of the concise detail-page plan.
2. Review of the complete assembled result.

Between these checkpoints, the Agent owns ordinary inspection, repair decisions, continuity calibration, and progression. Stop earlier only when a missing fact would make the work misleading, a new direction invalidates the approved plan, or the automatic repair budget is exhausted and user choice is required.

## Reference safety

- Reference pages teach composition, rhythm, proof form, density, color roles, and typography hierarchy.
- They do not authorize copying a product, brand, copy line, number, certificate, proprietary mascot, model likeness, or layout skeleton.
- Do not preserve screenshots' black borders, app chrome, watermarks, creator IDs, or capture artifacts.

## Current-product ownership

The current product must remain the visual anchor. Any person, hand, scene, icon, diagram, ingredient, material, or result exists to clarify this product's verified value. Remove unrelated decorative evidence.

## Stop conditions

Block planning or generation when:

- no usable current-product identity evidence exists;
- required numerical or regulatory claims have no source;
- conflicting facts cannot be resolved without user choice;
- the requested result depends on imitating a protected brand asset or a real person's likeness without authorization;
- the target platform profile is claimed as exact but cannot be verified.
